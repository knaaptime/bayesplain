    class bayesplain.teach.NaturalFrequencies(table, n, base_rate, sensitivity, specificity, case_label='cases', condition_label='the condition', flag_label='flagged')[source]¶

    A 2x2 grid of whole counts, and what it implies.

    Variables:¶
    table¶

    [[true positive, false negative], [false positive, true negative]] as integers.

    n¶

    Total number of imagined cases.

    base_rate¶

    The prevalence that was assumed.

    sensitivity¶

    The detection rate that was assumed.

    specificity¶

    The correct-clearance rate that was assumed.

    case_label='cases'¶

    What one row of the imagined data is.

    condition_label='the condition'¶

    The thing being detected.

    flag_label='flagged'¶

    What the detector does when it fires.

    Compute the same answer from the formula, for comparison.

    Returns:¶

    P(condition | flag) by Bayes’ rule. Agrees with posterior_given_flag up to the rounding needed to keep the grid in whole numbers, which is the point: the counting and the formula are the same operation.

    float

    property posterior_given_flag[source]¶

    Share of flagged cases that actually have the condition.

    This is the number the exercise is about, and it is obtained by dividing one count by another – no formula required.
